# Full-target licensed source-coverage matrix

## Status key

- **Candidate**: compatible reuse evidence exists, but claim extraction, field-level provenance, scientific review, and Arabic review are still required.
- **Blocked**: no compatible, field-level source has been identified in this pass.
- **Not approved**: source is unavailable for import despite possible research value.

No crop or claim type is fully covered or approved for import. All prepared records remain unpublished.

## Full-scope release status

The targets of 120+ plants/crops, 300+ diseases, and 150+ pests have **zero complete approved records**. The validation-batch plan and rollback controls are ready, but execution remains prohibited until the indicated field-level evidence and reviews are available.

## Vetted but excluded from reuse

- UC IPM is scientifically useful, but its legal notice prohibits commercial distribution and restricts reproduction; its photos also require permission. It is not an import authority.
- University of Minnesota Extension states that reproduction needs written permission. It is not an import authority.
- Egypt's Ministry of Agriculture website is geographically authoritative but asserts copyright without a compatible open licence. It is not an import authority.
