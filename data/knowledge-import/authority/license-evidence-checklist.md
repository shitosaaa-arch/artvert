# License evidence checklist

- [ ] Publisher and canonical source URL are documented.
- [ ] License text/version and commercial-use permission are documented.
- [ ] Modification, redistribution, attribution, and territory restrictions are documented.
- [ ] Citation and review date are recorded.
- [ ] Image owner, original URL, checksum, permitted use, attribution, and approval are recorded before any asset registration.
- [ ] Reject unsupported, unclear, or restricted licenses; do not substitute internet images.
