# First-batch source shortlist

1. **Plant taxonomy/names:** GBIF Backbone Taxonomy (CC BY 4.0) is the leading candidate, subject to source-registry approval and per-record citation. Kew POWO/WCVP is a high-authority cross-check but remains pending until reuse rights are documented.
2. **Disease, pest, deficiency, and management guidance:** no source is shortlisted as import-authoritative yet. Egypt/Arab-region government extension, public university, and plant-protection sources need a documented compatible license before review.
3. **Treatment guidance:** remains empty until country/label-dependent authority and reuse terms are approved. No generic chemical guidance or automatic ArtVert recommendation may be derived.
4. **Images:** only individual public-domain or CC BY/CC0 files with file-level evidence may enter the image-rights manifest; no candidate asset is approved.
5. **Products:** ArtVert internal data remains approved only for existing product-specific facts.
