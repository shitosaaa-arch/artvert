# Image source shortlist

- Individual Wikimedia Commons public-domain, CC0, or CC BY files may be evaluated one file at a time. Capture creator, original file URL, exact license, attribution, permitted use, checksum, linked entity, and approval.
- Do not rely on Commons collection-level statements, thumbnails, search results, or uploader identity as a rights manifest.
- No image asset is approved in this batch. Missing images do not block reviewed textual records and must never be treated as diagnostic evidence.
